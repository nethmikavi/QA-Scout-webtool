About QA Scout & Open Source

A lightweight, zero-paywall website quality engineering utility built for developers, QA interns, and small businesses.

🚀 How to Use QA Scout in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Run Website Audit: Enter your public URL in the audit box to scan for broken links, security headers, SEO, and accessibility issues.
  2. Review & Export Reports: Check your overall score out of 100, read actionable fix instructions, and export structured reports as JSON or CSV.
  3. Track & Compare Progress: Monitor past audits in Scan History or evaluate score improvements side-by-side using the Scan Comparison tool.

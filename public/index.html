Guideline: run a perfect audit

04 / STEPS

Follow these four steps to get a trustworthy score and a clear to-do list.

  1. 01

    Paste the exact page URL

    Use the page your visitors actually land on, such as your homepage, a landing page or a product page. Include https://. Only public pages work; localhost and private addresses are blocked.

  2. 02

    Run the scan and wait a few seconds

    QA Scout downloads the page once and checks links, security headers, SEO, accessibility and performance together. Most scans finish in under half a minute.

  3. 03

    Fix by severity, highest first

    Start with Critical issues, then Major, Medium and Minor. Use AI fix for a suggested solution, Log as bug to track it, or export the report to share with your team.

  4. 04

    Re-scan and compare

    Scan the same URL again after your changes. Open Scan Comparison to see your score move and which issues are gone.

  1. Run Website Audit: Enter your public URL in the audit box to scan for broken links, security headers, SEO, and accessibility issues.
  2. Review & Export Reports: Check your overall score out of 100, read actionable fix instructions, and export structured reports as JSON or CSV.
